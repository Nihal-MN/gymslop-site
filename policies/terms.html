Made to order

Every GYMSLOP piece is printed after you order it. Nothing sits in stock. That means production takes 2–5 business days before shipping, and it means a placed order cannot be cancelled once production has started.

Cancellation

Contact us within 2 hours of ordering and we will cancel and refund in full if production has not begun. After production starts, cancellation is not possible.

Defective, damaged or misprinted items

Send a photo of the item and the packaging within 14 days of delivery. We replace it free or refund in full. No return shipment is required for POD misprints. This is the fastest, no-argument route — use it.

Wrong size or colour ordered

We do not accept buyer's-remorse returns on made-to-order goods. Within the first 90 days we will offer a goodwill credit or a discounted replacement at our discretion. Check the size chart before ordering — it is on every listing.

UK / EU right of withdrawal ⚖️

Distance-selling rules generally give consumers 14 days to withdraw. Made-to-order goods are not automatically exempt — only personalised items are. Wording for these markets is pending legal review; until it is finalised, UK/EU buyers should contact us and we will resolve case by case.

Duties and taxes

UK and EU orders under the applicable thresholds have import VAT collected by the marketplace at checkout. US orders fulfilled domestically carry no import duty. For other destinations, any customs charge is the buyer's responsibility.
