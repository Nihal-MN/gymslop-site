Privacy

Draft v0 · last updated 28 Oct 2026

DRAFT — not legal advice. Written to match how the store actually works (Etsy as seller of record, Printify as producer). Review before relying on it in a dispute.

Who handles your data

Orders are placed on Etsy. Etsy is the seller of record and processes your payment, name, address and email. GYMSLOP designs the products; we do not receive or store your card details at any point.

What we receive

For each order we see what Etsy shares with a seller: the order contents, the shipping name and address, and any message you send us. That information is passed to our production partner so your item can be printed and shipped, and is retained in Etsy and the production partner's systems according to their policies.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent customer data.
  • We do not run advertising trackers on this page.
  • We do not store payment data — there is no checkout on this domain.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts. Etsy's marketplace pages set their own cookies, governed by Etsy's privacy policy.

Your rights

Requests to access, correct or delete your data should go to Etsy for marketplace records, and to us at hello@gymslop.fit for anything we hold directly. We answer within 30 days.
